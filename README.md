# AI-Assisted Business Workflows

Four administrative workflows designed for Saini IT Services, an academic IT support business scenario serving small businesses in Perth.

Developed for COM203 at Murdoch University, the project explores how AI can assist with summarising information, drafting documents and organising requests while retaining human judgement.

## Project Objective

Identify repetitive administrative tasks and design practical AI-assisted processes that support efficient work, accurate communication and responsible handling of client information.

## Workflows

| Workflow | AI assistance | Human responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Client enquiry and onboarding | Summarise client requirements, priorities and questions requiring clarification | Check privacy, verify the summary and determine suitable services |
| Service proposal and quote drafting | Structure an initial proposal using verified requirements | Confirm technical scope, correct assumptions and determine pricing |
| Support ticket management | Summarise requests and draft responses | Investigate the issue, decide the technical action and approve the response |
| Client follow-up and service reporting | Convert service notes into draft reports and follow-up messages | Verify completed work, check recommendations and approve communication |

## 1. Client Enquiry and Onboarding

Tools: Google Forms, ChatGPT and Microsoft Word.

1. Collect the client's business details, IT requirements and current issues.
2. Remove unnecessary sensitive or confidential information.
3. Use ChatGPT to summarise requirements, priorities and clarification questions.
4. Compare the summary with the original enquiry.
5. Apply human judgement to identify suitable services.
6. Prepare a verified client brief in Word.

## 2. Service Proposal and Quote Drafting

Tools: ChatGPT and Microsoft Word.

1. Review the verified client brief.
2. Identify the proposed services and scope.
3. Use ChatGPT to produce a structured proposal draft.
4. Check technical accuracy and unsupported assumptions.
5. Determine and verify pricing through human review.
6. Prepare the final proposal and quote in Word.

AI assists with drafting; final service recommendations and pricing remain human decisions.

## 3. Support Ticket Management

Tools: Email, ChatGPT and Microsoft Word.

1. Receive a client's support request.
2. Screen the request for unnecessary confidential information.
3. Use ChatGPT to summarise the issue and relevant details.
4. Investigate the issue and determine the appropriate technical action.
5. Use verified information to draft a response with AI assistance.
6. Review and approve the response before sending.

The workflow retains human responsibility for diagnosis and technical advice.

## 4. Client Follow-Up and Service Reporting

Tools: ChatGPT and Microsoft Word.

1. Record the work completed and relevant service notes.
2. Remove unnecessary sensitive information.
3. Use ChatGPT to organise the notes into a draft service report.
4. Check the draft against the original notes.
5. Prepare an AI-assisted follow-up message.
6. Review and approve the report and message.

## Responsible AI Design

Each workflow incorporates checkpoints for:

- Privacy: provide only the information needed for the task.
- Accuracy: compare AI-generated content with the source material.
- Unsupported assumptions: correct invented or unjustified details.
- Fairness: review outputs for biased or unfair assumptions.
- Human judgement: retain control over technical decisions and pricing.
- Approval: review client-facing content before it is used.

AI-generated material is treated as a draft requiring verification.

## Project Scope

This is an academic workflow design and demonstration project.

The processes involve manual information transfer and human review. The report does not establish a deployed autonomous agent system or automated integration between the tools.

Efficiency benefits are proposed outcomes; the project does not report measured time savings.

## Practical Relevance

The project demonstrates an approach to using AI for routine administrative work while preserving accountability.

Its summarisation, report-drafting and verification methods are relevant to workplaces that need to turn operational information into clear, checked documents.
